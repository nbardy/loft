## The style (use in every prompt)
Japanese ukiyo-e woodblock print in the manner of Hokusai's waves. White water is flat cream with bold black key lines of varied weight, built from a few large rolling masses that break into curled spiral heads and clawed fingers, with small round droplets flung beyond; never a web, never noise, never soft or airbrushed. The sea is flat deep indigo with thin cream wave lines. A brick-red crab leg, claw or a small wooden boat with blue sails where a body is needed. No text, numbers or labels. Shapes simple enough to draw with a few procedural curves.
