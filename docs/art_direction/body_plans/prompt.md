## The characters

Five colossal crabs, each the size of a small island, standing in shallow sea, drawn as a Japanese woodblock print (flat inks, bold black key lines, brick red, cream, indigo, moss green, a few accents). They dance together in a line, so each must be recognisable as a pure black silhouette, by proportion and carriage alone, before any face or paint:

1. THE FIDDLER: slight and tall. A small dark rounded body lifted high on long thin legs; one enormous banner-like great claw (longer than its whole body) raised on the right; a tiny cutter claw; bug eyes on very tall periscope stalks; a small ruined lighthouse on its back.
2. THE SAMURAI CRAB: squat, broad and heavy. A wide low flat carapace almost sitting on the water, carried very low; short thick armoured legs set wide and sprawling; two matched heavy gauntlet claws held low in front; a scowling war-mask face on the front of its shell; a small castle keep with two pines on its back.
3. THE HERMIT CRAB: lives in a tall coiled sea-snail shell. The shell is big and tall: its banded dome and at least one turn of its coil rise high above and behind the crab's head, so from the front you see the shell towering over the head. The body comes out of the shell's mouth: low at the front, rising back into the shell. Its walking legs fan well forward, splayed out in front of it like a hand on a table; one great claw carried across its front; eyes on stalks.
4. THE BRUISER: big-bodied and top-heavy, leaning forward like a boxer. A huge deep carapace, front lower than the back; thick heavy forelegs braced in front, shorter rear legs; one great claw held out wide to its side; heavy brow and big head; a small green island with cliffs on its back.
5. THE SPIDER CRAB: a small spiny body very high on extremely long thin stilt legs; thin tongs; a small red shrine on its back.

Style clause (repeat for every picture): Japanese woodblock print, flat colour areas, bold black outlines, limited palette of brick red, cream, indigo, moss green and black, clean readable shapes, plain background.
