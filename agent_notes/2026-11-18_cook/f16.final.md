Implemented opt-in `--precision mixed` for builds, renders, the viewer, and `loft serve`. Dawn and Rust/wgpu negotiate `shader-f16`. Version-4 artifacts carry both variants, and devices without the feature report `f32-fallback`. Serve records the selected precision alongside `tier`.

On Apple M4 at 1280×800, median GPU times from f32 to mixed were **3.539 → 3.539 ms** for Serpent, **5.767 → 5.767 ms** for PBR, and **3.801 → 4.260 ms** for the soft medium. Peak RGB difference was **2/255**. The PBR palette shrank from **4,096 to 2,048 bytes**. f32 remains the default. Geometry, marching, time, depth, and the medium’s interleaved source tables use f32.

Three baseline frames and two build directories matched byte for byte. Typecheck, the focused GPU test, CLI checks, and Rust rendering passed.
