## Your lane: G1, src/expr.ts's compiler half in Rust

**Target.** Write `rust/crates/loft-lower/src/graph/` (split it into files as you like, under that directory). It
holds an Expr arena that reproduces src/expr.ts exactly.

- **Constructors and interning** (`Expr.make` keys; `calls`/`vecs`/`gets`/`scalars` tables; SameValueZero for `-0`):
  - every prim, with its type-widening, splat rules and the identities `add`/`mul` apply (`isZero`, `isOne`);
  - `withIdentities` mode;
  - `lift`, `vecOf`, `get` (swizzle), `lib`, `fetch`, `row`, `variable`, `input`, `param`, `iterate` (loops).
- **`fold`**: the f32 constant folding, expr.ts ~1544-1660, `Math.fround` per operation.

  recorded over binders and substituted equals TS's direct call (PLAN.md gap 18).

- **`emitWgsl`** and `wgslNode`:
  - `INFIX`, `SPLAT`;
  - `select`'s argument order;
  - `mod` expanded;
  - `roundEven` written as `round`;
  - the loop statements.
- **Names** follow lane P0's rule: in one call, lets are `${prefix}${k}` and loops `${prefix}L${j}`, numbered
  in emission order. Read `agent_notes/2026-11-19_port/P0.md` if it exists; P0's rule wins.
- **The three literal spellings**, exactly as JS spells them:
  - expr.ts `lit`: the shortest `toPrecision(p)` that rounds to the same f32, with JS's ties;
  - scene_wgsl.ts `lit`: `${x}.0` or `String(x)`;
  - recur.ts `lit`.

  Also JS `Number.prototype.toString`, `toFixed` (ties, `-0`) and `toPrecision`, in `graph/lit.rs`. Lanes B, S and D

- **Import from the wire.** Build an arena from the decoded job (`Input::graph()`, loft-ir's Graph) through your

## Context (read first)

Loft (`~/git/loft`) is a 3D rendering library. A scene, written in TypeScript (run by Bun), is compiled into an
**artefact**: a directory with `manifest.json`, `wgsl/` and `bin/` (`docs/artefact_format.md`). `rust/crates/loft-loader`
draws it. We are porting the TS compiler's **pure lowering** to Rust, **1:1**, in the crate `rust/crates/loft-lower`.
"1:1" means the same algorithms translated, with every byte of output identical to the TS compiler's on the whole
corpus (125 examples and 170 seeds). The TS side keeps running the author's closures.

- The plan is `agent_notes/2026-11-19_port/PLAN.md`. Read its sections 2 (the cut and the gaps), 3 (the harness), 4
  (your lane's row and its neighbours) and 6 (risks).
- The harness is `tools/port/diff.ts`, with `tools/port/sections.ts` (sections and owners) and `tools/port/ts_side.ts`
  (what the TS side writes). Read all three before writing code: your gate is that harness scoped to you.
- Read `AGENTS.md` and `CORE.md`.
