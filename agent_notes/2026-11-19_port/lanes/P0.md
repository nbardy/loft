## Your lane: P0, canonical WGSL names in the TS compiler

**Blocker.**

- Every let the TS compiler emits is named `${prefix}${expr.id}`, and every loop `${prefix}L${loop.id}`
  (`src/expr.ts` `emitWgsl`, lines ~1076-1118).
- `expr.id` counts every node the process ever made: imports, discarded work, earlier variants.
- Run `bun tools/port/diff.ts --mode shift --only examples__bead`. It makes 1000 throwaway nodes before the scene, and
  every chunk changes.
- No Rust translation can reproduce those names. Nothing byte-identical is possible until they follow the scene
  alone.

**Target: the naming rule.** Lane G1 implements it in Rust in parallel. If you must deviate, write the rule you chose
at the top of `agent_notes/2026-11-19_port/P0.md`, in its first hour.

- **Lets.** In one `emitWgsl` call, a let is `${prefix}${k}`. `k` counts the lets that call has written, from 0,
  in emission order, loop bodies included.
- **Loops.** A loop is `${prefix}L${j}`. `j` counts the loops that call has opened, from 0. Its state, index and
  next names keep their current shapes (`${L}s${j}`, `${L}i`, `${L}n${j}`).
- **`pre`.** Names passed in `pre` keep theirs.
- **One scope.** Two `emitWgsl` calls that write into one WGSL scope must not collide. Check every call site:
  - src/compile/bind.ts:154;
  - src/compile/tables.ts:717, 736, 740, 741;
  - src/compile/compute.ts:70;
  - src/compile/recur.ts:40-41;
  - src/text/index.ts:365.

  If any two share a scope with one prefix, give them distinct prefixes, and say which in your note.
- **Other names.** Hunt every other name that follows a process-global counter and reaches artefact bytes. Candidates:
  - `nextProgram` (`_T${id}r${k}`, tables.ts:175, 204);
  - `lamp${count++}` (src/frame/lamp_shadow.ts:76);
  - `Row.next` (src/expr.ts:30, if a row id reaches text);
  - raw library names hashed from text that holds such names (src/text/index.ts `libOf`; this follows once lets are
    canonical).

  The shift run finds them.

**Files you may change:** `src/expr.ts` (`emitWgsl` and its loop naming), and the call sites above, only where they
collide or name by a counter. Nothing else under `src/`.

**Expected effect.**

- Artefact bytes change once: the let names are renumbered, and the chunk names follow.
- Pictures do not change: this is a renaming.
- Commit with a message that says the artefact bytes change and the pictures do not (AGENTS.md freeze rule).

## Context (read first)

Loft (`~/git/loft`) is a 3D rendering library. A scene, written in TypeScript (run by Bun), is compiled into an
**artefact**: a directory with `manifest.json`, `wgsl/` and `bin/` (`docs/artefact_format.md`). `rust/crates/loft-loader`
draws it. We are porting the TS compiler's **pure lowering** to Rust, **1:1**, in the crate `rust/crates/loft-lower`.
"1:1" means the same algorithms translated, with every byte of output identical to the TS compiler's on the whole
corpus (125 examples and 170 seeds). The TS side keeps running the author's closures.

- The plan is `agent_notes/2026-11-19_port/PLAN.md`. Read its sections 2 (the cut and the gaps), 3 (the harness), 4
  (your lane's row and its neighbours) and 6 (risks).
- The harness is `tools/port/diff.ts`, with `tools/port/sections.ts` (sections and owners) and `tools/port/ts_side.ts`
  (what the TS side writes). Read all three before writing code: your gate is that harness scoped to you.
- Read `AGENTS.md` and `CORE.md`.
