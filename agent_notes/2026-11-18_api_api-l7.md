## What changed

- **`loft check --explain`** (`src/cli/explain.ts`, new). For each ranged check that does not hold, or the closest one
  when all hold, at its witness: the check's own line; the comparison that decides the margin there (walking
  `and`/`or`/`not` the way `marginOf` defines the margin); both sides' values; the nearest nodes under it written on
  other lines, with values; and the `loft render ... --time t [--param ...] [--variant v] --views <first camera>`
  command for the witness. Every site is quoted from its file. Values come from the interval `Evaluator` on a point box,
  so a param-ranged witness is evaluated as the search saw it.
- **Sites for call form.** `src/expr.ts`: `recordStacks()` / `stackOf(e)`, a WeakMap from node to the `Error` made at
  `Expr.fresh` (now the only constructor path), and `Error.stackTraceLimit` raised to 40 while recording.
  `src/errors.ts`: `sitesOf(stack)` (every author frame, innermost first); `siteOf` is its first.
- **`--at time=1.37,name=v`**: `pinned(check, at, probe)` in `src/ranged.ts`, a sum `pinned | outside`. `ranged()` was
  split so its tail (`fromFormula`) builds a check from a classified formula; settings are kept per formula, so a copy
  of a check (solve's `rangedHold` spreads one) still pins. Names no check ranges over: usage error listing the names.
- **A fix found by `--at`**: a nested search under a one-point range ran as a box (few splits) and stopped at "width 0"
  with loose bounds (`--at stone.speed=5` on `examples/ranged.ts`: [0.0165, 0.02]). A point range is now evaluated as a
  point: [0.01993, 0.02000], finished. Unpinned results unchanged (`examples/ranged.ts`: same bounds, same 3685 boxes).
- **Summary line** counts proofs and sampled passes apart (L2's open item): `24 held, 0 found no failure in samples,
  2 failed`. `tests/ranged.test.ts` updated to it.
- Fixture `tests/fixtures/explain/heron_rail_raised.ts` (the heron solved for its rail, checked against the rail 0.2 m
  higher); `holds` is now exported from `examples/second/heron_leap.ts` for it. Test `tests/explain.test.ts` (2 tests).
- Docs: `docs/guide.md` (checks section), `docs/errors.md` (ranged checks), `DESIGN.md` (new 2.7 subsection), the CLI

## Where I went off the design, and why

- **No `--sites` flag.** Recording costs about half a compile, and `--explain` is the only reader; a second flag would
  buy nothing. `--explain` records.
- **No percentages ("82%").** I found no share with a definition a reader could act on; the deciding comparison, its
  two sides and the values of what it reads say the same thing exactly.
- **Interned constants and leaves are never cited**: their recorded line is wherever an equal node was first built.
- **JavaScriptCore drops tail-call frames**, so `return length(...)` in a helper is cited at the caller's line (the
  heron's `apart` result is at :87, its inner `sub` at :43). Stated in DESIGN.md.
- `--at` takes leaf labels only (`time=`, not `t=`): one spelling, and the error lists the labels.

## Left

- `--at` cannot set a param no check ranges over (`Probe` holds params at their defaults; `src/check.ts`).
- Sampled checks (skins, `budget`, ...) are not explained; their rows are what there is.
- An undecided check with no witness gets one line ("nothing to explain at a point").
