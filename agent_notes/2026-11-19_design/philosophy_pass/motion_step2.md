# Motion after the foot lands

## What the author writes

**A crab reverses on a ferry.** The deck is moving, the crab receives steering from the player, and its feet remember where they landed. This author wants the urgent steps low. Here is their entire change to the scrambling crab, including installation of the new law.

```ts
const lift = wgsl`
  fn lift(u: f32, urgency: f32) -> f32 {
    let arch = 16.0 * u * u * (1.0-u) * (1.0-u);
    return arch * mix(0.12, 0.04, urgency);
  }`.lift;
const feet = steps(120, crab.start, scramble(crab, deck, steer, lift));
drawCrab(crab, carriage(crab, feet), feet);
```

**A heron follows a fish, then looks across the rail.** The fish path is known for this shot. The animator owns the instant at which it loses the bird's attention. The eyelook and skull are separate controls. On this take the feet and takeoff have already been approved.

```ts
const body = hop(heron, keys, rail, landing.at(1.2));
const interest = wgsl`
  fn interest(t: f32) -> f32 {
    return 1.0 - smoothstep(0.45, 0.60, t);

const target = mix(ahead(body), fish, interest(time));
const neck = reachNeck(heron.neck, body, target, horizon);
drawHeron(heron, aimEyes(neck, target), body);
```

**A boss takes a second hit during its guard.** The game supplies the point and impulse in world coordinates. The designer gives the left shoulder a physical response and keeps the face in the directed guard. Here the reaction engine's angular impulse law is the part the author writes.

```ts
const kick = wgsl`
  fn kick(point: vec3f, com: vec3f, impulse: vec3f) -> vec3f {
    return cross(point - com, impulse);
  }`.kick;
const shoulder = steps(120, titan.rest, recoil(titan.leftArm, hits, kick));
```

**An engine small enough to replace outright.** This is another author's locomotion engine for a clockwork prop on the same deck. It has a rigid shell and one telescoping strut. Both ends are visible. The shell rises and falls while the pad stays bolted to the ferry. The whole engine is a function from time and a support transform to drawable geometry.

```ts
function jack(t, deck) {
  const pad = deck.point([0, 0, 0]);
  const height = wgsl`
    fn height(t: f32) -> f32 { return 0.5 + 0.2 * cos(t); }
  `.height(t);
  return { pad, shell: add(pad, deck.vector(vec3(0, height, 0))) };
}
drawJack(jack(time, deck));
```

Three observations decide this design. At the instant two trajectories diverge because of a past landing, their common current inputs cannot determine the next pose. Store the part of the past the law needs. At the instant a foot lands, three coordinates the animator was free to draw in the air acquire a simpler description, one point fixed in the support frame. Change coordinates there. At the instant a solver, skin attachment or animator changes the toe, a statement about that toe must follow the change all the way to the picture. A good core gives all three authors access to the same graph, buffers and evidence. The choice of the next step belongs to each animal. ## Begin with the observable

Take a named point on the underside of a crab's distal segment. Let its local coordinates be p, its final attachment transform F(t), its support's rigid transform D(t), and the point recorded at touchdown a, in deck coordinates. The plant error in metres is ‖D(t)⁻¹ F(t)p − a‖. This gives three independent programs a common question. The stepper chooses when the interval begins and ends. The rig supplies F and the same p the drawing uses. The checker evaluates their composition. A deck translating 0.3 m/s carries a correct foot 6 cm in 0.2 s. A checker measuring world-space stillness would complain most about the foot doing exactly the right thing.

Represent the stance by a and the identity of D. In flight capture the outgoing point and incoming landing in their declared frames and give the artist a path between them. In the example arch above, u runs from zero to one over the swing. Height and its first derivative vanish at both ends. The midpoint rises exactly 4 cm at maximum urgency and 12 cm at minimum urgency. A hand-drawn stamp can use a nonzero arrival velocity. If the support itself is moving, match its velocity at attachment where the shot requires a smooth catch. A smoothstep in world space with zero end velocity meets a moving deck with a velocity discontinuity. Interpolating the offset in deck space, or matching the end velocities in a Hermite span, gives different, editable arrivals.

Holding a point removes up to three positional freedoms. It leaves rotation about that point free. A claw clamped around a handle may also need its palm normal fixed and twist limited. Two rigid pins do not fully determine rotation about their common line. The author can use that remaining rotation to keep the elbow's silhouette. This is a reason to build a pose from the information known in each interval. Propagating seven world transforms forward and asking a late solver to rediscover one exact contact at every sample throws away information the author already supplied.

The construction can fail. For a two-link chain of lengths l₁ and l₂, the distance d from hip to target must lie in [|l₁−l₂|, l₁+l₂] even before joint limits. Within that annulus the knee lies on a circle. For d>0 its axial coordinate from the hip is (d²+l₁²−l₂²)/(2d), and its distance off the axis is the square root of l₁² minus that coordinate squared. A pole direction chooses a point on the circle. A nearly straight knee needs a retained or authored bend direction to avoid a flip. The equal-length folded case d=0 needs its own construction. Record that choice, including which previous bend is required, in the chain function. Outside reach, return the gap, offending site and attempted target. In an offline take the author can move the root or change a key. A live law can release the pin or draw at attainable reach and publish the residual. The renderer can display all three performances. Only the first one that actually meets the plant deserves a successful plant check.

The invention I propose is to make **the same observed attachment the output of a construction, an input to its independent check, and a dependency root for compiling a replacement**. Pose construction may eliminate coordinates during one interval and hand them back at a boundary. A change of solver, time representation or backend is allowed so long as the consumer supplies the next pose and declares what history it reads. The domain library can carry the observed point through these changes and the compiler can discard everything irrelevant to the chosen output. Coordinate elimination, partial evaluation and contact IK all have precedents. The research claim is the combination surviving an arbitrary consumer rewrite with useful evidence and competitive frame cost. The claim fails if specializing the standard controller is the only way to obtain the fast path.

## Interfaces narrow enough to throw away

The meta engine needs expressions, calls, loops, typed table rows, kernels with explicit reads, writes and evaluation time, and ordered GPU work on a borrowed device. On the host it needs a way to supply time and inputs and to request identified outputs. Build can evaluate, differentiate and bound a supported expression, or ask an ordinary numerical algorithm for coefficients. A bundle is a runnable result. The stable runtime can load one without knowing how a knee bends. Render and light read the same published frames as the motion drawing. A shadow caster that samples last frame's claw would break the shot even if the colour pass is correct. Each reader declares the resource version it needs. [Rust bundle and kernel types](../../../rust/crates/loft-ir/src/bundle.rs) · [Embedding contract](../../../docs/keel.md)

I would ship **one motion sub meta engine**. It supplies a body with named local attachments, a pose mapping those attachments into the shot, and functions to express residuals on that pose over a domain. It uses the core's existing choices of time and storage. A solver receives numerical unknowns, graph functions and limits and returns numbers or a named failure. A stepped law receives previous state and the inputs for one tick and returns next state. A builder receives values and emits the graph or pass list its consumer will run. These are sufficient interfaces for several independent engines. None requires deriving a character class, registering a behaviour, obtaining a world singleton or assigning a priority to every joint.

The orchestration is ordinary source. For the crab the recipe reads the previous anchors and steering, picks a support to unload, updates its swing, fits a carriage over the remaining supports, constructs the legs and publishes their frames. It may need carriage prediction *before* choosing which foot to lift. Expose that prediction as an argument and the order as calls. If two parts need to settle together, the author can put both inside a bounded local iteration and return its residual. A cycle between two current resources has no defined execution. Bind the previous version at a declared tick or supply a joint solve. A topological sort is a good implementation of a dependency. Sorting two writers by a magic limb priority would introduce an acting decision.

The library can grow. It should contain readable two-link construction, spine frames, support selection, finite splines, exact spring response on specified polynomial spans, fixed-step integration, contact projections, simple force drives, importer adapters and a sampled readout. Each belongs with a picture and a consumer that uses it. For a piecewise polynomial driver of a linear spring, precompute the initial position and velocity for every span by propagating its exact solution. A seek then evaluates one span with its own boundary data. A streamed driver supplies history at ticks. Putting the same spring in both shots does not make the stream seekable. Expose both functions and their preconditions. A person adding a nonlinear tendon can keep the integrator and replace the force expression.

Expose the numerics below those calls too. The current Rust solve commits its unknowns to uniform cubic B-splines, its preferences to three weights and its Newton updates to the implementation in one module. Keep that whole method as a reproducible library solver. For consumers, expose evaluation of values and requested derivative rows on a tape and construction of curves/tables from returned numbers. A Rust build tool can submit another family's rows, use its own factorization, then hand standard coefficient resources to the recorder/build path. A TS author can also supply numbers from a separate tool. No runtime plugin or arbitrary native callback in a distributed bundle is necessary. A replacement objective with a different family changes the curve sampled by the checker. Give that curve explicitly to the check job. Evidence constrained to the outgoing spline ceases at its boundary. [Current solver](../../../rust/crates/loft-core/src/solve.rs) · [Recorded solve declarations](../../../src/record/decls.ts)

For example, the read and write edges of the scrambling recipe can be read straight down. Each name on the right denotes data or an ordinary function supplied by the engine author.

```text
before       = state[k-1]
choice       = release(rig, before, input[k], deck[k])
state[k]     = advance(before, choice, input[k], deck[k])
targets      = footPoints(state[k], deck[k])
shell        = fitCarriage(rig, targets, input[k])
frames       = legs(rig, shell, targets)
observedToe  = transform(frames[distal], rig.sites[toe].localFrame)
```

Compile the first three lines as one recurrence and the last four as a pose calculation or inlined reads. At a hold the contact coordinates are data in the previous state. Changing only `fitCarriage` keeps that recurrence reusable. If the new release rule reads yesterday's actual shell height, the dependency now crosses the tick boundary. Extend state with that height and invalidate checkpoints from the older layout. A checker can reuse a result only when its entire observation, quantified domain, arithmetic and inputs are identical or when a verified equivalence covers the substitution. Retaining the name of the toe establishes correspondence for comparison. It cannot establish equivalence of two bent legs.

The fast path is public as well. A consumer can author a single raw WGSL workgroup for all of one animal's contacts, publish normal pose rows, then use all of Loft's ordinary draws. Another consumer can keep the recorder and replace the schedule builder with their own ordered raw passes. The low-level emitter/runtime validates dependencies, layouts, origins, resource lifetimes and device limits and executes that list. Declare opaque resource effects conservatively and keep author bounds checks. The existing validator and typed errors are valuable here. The only privilege a supplied crab should have is that its author has already debugged it. A custom draw that displaces the visible foot supplies the displacement to the observed point, or the available report ends at the undeformed skeleton. ## Data the next author can read

Use the following logical shape in the library. This is design notation for ordinary sum types, functions and arrays. It introduces no second VM or serialized body language. A body asset can be produced by a modeller, by ten lines of parametric code, or by reading a rig from elsewhere.

```text
Segment = { id, parent: Root | Child(parentId, joint, restFrame) }
Joint   = Fixed | Hinge(axis, angleInterval) | Ball(restBasis, swingCone, twistInterval)
Site    = { id, segment, localFrame }
Rig     = { segments, sites }
Frame   = { translation: vec3, rotation: unitQuat }
Pose    = segmentId -> Frame
Pin     = { site, supportSite, pointInSupport, interval }
Foot    = Planted(supportSite, pointInSupport, sinceTick)
        | Swing(startInWorld, endInSupport, supportSite, firstTick, durationTicks, height)
```

Here Site means an anatomical attachment. Keep its source location separately using the existing IR site. The last type is an example controller's state. A swimmer should delete it. The jack uses ordinary points and never allocates a segment table. A rigid pose is a frame from segment local space to world space. Rest frames on edges are parent-relative. Each library boundary says which convention it accepts. Use metres, seconds, kilograms, radians, right-handed frames and a documented quaternion component order, say xyzw. Quaternions must be normalizable. Pick the continuous sign for interpolation, including across clip boundaries. Scale and shear used for the squash of an eye are art deformations. Where these affect a checked landmark, evaluate the actual deformation there. Using a unit quaternion as their representation would lose information.

Validate parent existence, the rooted forest, unique site names, unit axes and finite limits once at import. Resolve names to dense indices. Topologically order by dependency and retain a reverse map for errors and export. A closed linkage uses a tree plus explicit constraints closing the loop. Mass, centre of mass and inertia tensors are per-segment data required by laws that read them. A graphic strut needs none. A rigid-body law requires positive mass and a physically admissible inertia about the declared centre, with a declared frame. A dynamic law given a decorative antenna without inertia can point to that antenna at build. Kinematic and driven segments can coexist. Declare the kinematic driver as a boundary input to the driven subsystem, including velocity when its mechanics needs it.

“Foot”, “eye” and “grasper” are sets of named sites in the example body data. A site can occur in more than one set. A generic walking recipe uses supporting sites, rest offsets, allowable reach and bending directions. A consumer can supply edges between supports that may not lift together. A library routine can derive those edges from adjacency in the particular rest layout. Keep that routine editable and use its output in both stepping and reporting. A six-legged crab should require six actual homes and chain descriptions, zero edits to indexing expressions, and zero changes to the checker. A creature with wheels or a continuous belly has a different contact law. Its author can read the same sites and write a rolling or distributed-support recipe. Predict quality on a named held-out morphology with a stated feasible task. A skeleton alone cannot determine whether its owner stalks or skips.

Pack physical data for its reader. For the first crab use two vec4s per rigid output frame, translation plus padding and a quaternion, with the same attachment function in draw and export. One body and eight three-piece legs use 25 frames, 800 bytes. One slot per foot has three vec4s. In stance these carry the local anchor and support identity, mode and tick information. In swing the same footprint carries start, end, height, support, first tick and duration. Keep integer tags and ticks exact. A bounded V1 fixture can use exactly representable small integers in f32 lanes. Long sessions need integer storage or a bounded local epoch and an integer host origin. At 120 Hz a global f32 integer tick counter exhausts consecutive integers after about 38.8 hours. A long-running gameplay contract should take integer ticks and reconstruct a small local time for the shader. A translation and orientation plus linear and angular velocity cost four more root vec4s. Keeping root and feet gives 448 bytes per actor state. A narrower law can omit unused rows.

A constraint is a signed margin, the truth test at zero, units, a domain and a binding to the final observed site or curve. The margin for a plant within ε is ε minus the deck-space displacement. The gaze error is an angle between the final optical axis and eye-to-target direction. Define what the law does when target and eye coincide, where a direction is undefined. Skull roll is a second margin. Joint range and deck clearance are others. An instant, a closed interval of a baked shot, a half-open stance ending on a release tick, and a finite list of rendered samples are different domains. Encode which one the report quantified. Split held intervals at an event. At the shared endpoint evaluate whichever state the boundary rule presents and check continuity separately where requested.

Reuse Rust's verdict, witness, work budget and proof scope. Builds leave solve holds `Unproved`. A ranged `loft check` over supported f32 graph operations can prove the margin, find a counterexample, or return an undecided reason. Sampled game traces should report the number of observations, worst residual, tick, site, precision, input identity and units. A GPU-only function may yield an excellent sampled trace and an unsupported-operation error if asked for an interval proof. If the author supplies a CPU twin, agreement at sample points is evidence about those points. A proof about the shader requires a common checked lowering or an established error bound between the programs. Preserve the origin of trajectory, solver, draw attachment and report. This matters when a two-centimetre wrist slip occurs only between two of a generator's hard keyframes. ## Four engines built from these pieces

**Scramble and recover.** A small support controller can begin each tick by asking where the body would be if nobody stepped. For level ground at roughly constant centre-of-mass height h, use the linear inverted pendulum estimate c = x + v√(h/g) in the horizontal plane. With h = 0.9 m and g = 9.81 m/s² the lookahead is 0.303 s. At 0.6 m/s the point is 18.2 cm ahead of the mass centre. A reachable place near c is a candidate footfall. The estimate assumes a model of support and height. On a ferry, express position and relative velocity in its moving frame and account for acceleration when the law needs it. A geometric hull check on a rapidly rotating deck gives a geometric result. It cannot certify available friction or tipping dynamics.

The controller can rank candidates by displacement from their preferred landing and by the support margin after removal. Compute the convex hull of the remaining points projected into a specified support plane and the signed distance to its boundary. Collinear supports need their line or point case. Choose the admissible candidate with the largest score and break exact ties by stable site identity. A single decision on a common previous snapshot prevents two neighbours each seeing permission to lift. In a scramble that needs two feet to lift, choose the pair explicitly and evaluate the remaining hull. When no feasible placement exists the law may stagger, grasp with the big claw, or fall. A claimed recovery requires successful later support and a picture of the transfer. Expose the unsuccessful attempt too. Step 1's baked turn had slack in every reach constraint and looked dead because nothing asked the carriage to transfer weight.

Phase 1 below uses a displacement trigger on a level deck. In the fuller engine the selected support and a desired acceleration can also drive body lean and asymmetric compression. For a quasi-static planar load solve for nonnegative normal loads with the right net force and moment. Double support can leave a family of distributions. Let the artist choose which leg appears to take the strain or use compliance to determine it. For frictional dynamics test the tangential force against μ times normal force. Slipping is an outcome with a different law for the anchor. The picture can also use a purely authored lean. Its provenance says exactly how the lean was obtained. Timing the kinematic version against a general contact solver only has meaning at comparable picture and constraint quality.

**Direct a performance.** Use authored anticipation and carriage keys for the approach, a ballistic centre of mass in free flight where that is the direction, then a directed landing with supports. The head and eyes can continue a fixation through trunk rotation. A simple gaze recipe first evaluates the eye's location, aims at a target, chooses skull up independently, and assigns residual bearing beyond the eye limits to the neck. Near a parallel up and sight direction, keep a previous side axis or use a specified pole. A fully extended neck can request torso yaw. The author decides whether the torso can yield while the feet are planted and whether a glance ends when the fish is lost. If eye position changes as the neck bends, recompute aim from that final eye position. Report fixation error from there. In the call site, `ahead(body)` constructs a point forward at the current eye height on the level-head track, and `horizon` sets the skull’s up reference. The animator edits `interest(t)`. Its edit can leave the landing and every approved foot curve intact if the neck is downstream of them. Turning the torso to prolong fixation makes those curves dependencies again.

For the solve, let an animator supply keys as preferences and choose degrees of freedom per interval. A hard hold of the face can eliminate it from the unknowns throughout that span. Squared jerk is a serviceable smoothness term with an inexpensive integral on cubic splines. If a shot needs effort, give the consumer a build function for residuals in physical units. In planar rotation around a fixed joint, τ = Iα − τ_external. In a multibody landing the generalized torque includes coupled inertia, velocity and gravitational terms and the generalized contact force Jᵀf. An effort integral needs the contact forces or their distribution as unknowns or inputs, a scale for each actuator, and an explicit quadrature rule. The four-point rule used for squared spline derivatives has no automatic exactness for a nonlinear torque. Publish the particular model along with the motion. The heron can get its glance back by changing a target in a few tokens, long before any torque calculation pays for itself. [Heron look rounds and owner feedback](../../../../_wt/loft-motion/agent_notes/2026-11-19_motion.md) · [Existing struck holds](../../../src/geom/struck.ts)

**Interrupt a performance.** Keel timestamps a hit with a sequence number, target, point and impulse. A fixed-tick law consumes each event once in a defined order, including multiple impulses in one tick. With a lever arm of 0.4 m perpendicular to 10 N·s the angular impulse is 4 N·m·s. A body with principal inertia 2 kg·m² about that axis gains 2 rad/s in the isolated impulse test, before any joint or support reaction. A coincident opposite impulse through the same point cancels it. An opposite impulse 0.12 s later acts on a changed body. An example engine can integrate and damp the driven links, interpolate their attachment to a keyed guard, and run a bounded contact projection. Put stiffness, row order, inverse inertia and how the guard resumes in that engine. A heavier shoulder should show its changed response even if the face stays stone still. Overwriting the posed shoulder only for rendering creates an artificial jump when physics next takes control. At that handoff initialize the driven pose and velocity from what was presented, or show the intended discontinuity explicitly.

**Acquire a performance.** Normalize clips at the edge into joint ids, parent-relative rest and animated frames, source basis, units, sample times and interpolation. A missing correspondence gets a named mapping error or an explicitly chosen rest channel. Retarget rest-relative rotations by changing basis, scale translations in declared units, and fit the root path in world or support space as specified. A grasper-to-grasper correspondence can cross different numbers of joints. Preserve the semantic endpoint and use the target creature's chain and pole to choose among solutions. Foot contacts from a model are proposed intervals. Correct on the target rig, then check the final toe, grip and timing. A live source uses the same evaluation of attachments on a timestamped pose buffer. Decide how to handle a missing sample in the stream contract, for example hold the last sample and report its age. An offline mesh skin, procedural sweep and readback all consume the resulting pose. Export includes timestamps, rig identity, basis, units and parent-relative or world convention. With both endpoints included a one-second clip at 60 Hz has 61 poses. Read back asynchronous batches only when a tool requests them. Keel's normal embedded rendering has zero pose readbacks. Each example admits cuts at different depths. Change the value of the swing polynomial and the compiled shader changes. Replace the allocator of footfalls and the deck attachment and checker survive. Replace the whole tick with a workgroup and only the published frames and resource dependencies need survive. Import externally simulated poses and retain just the draw and readout. Replace even that drawing with a ray-program taking two endpoints as in the jack. A cold reader should be able to locate the exact source of the selected support and the expression that bends the knee from the observed toe and tick. Have `loft show` print that dependency path with the actual kernel and authored source sites. Generate the signatures and layouts supplied to `loft context` from those same declarations. This lets another model edit a real implementation without learning a parallel vocabulary of configuration keys.

## What to compile and what to remember

Motion has two independent time questions. When does a value become available, and how much history does reproducing it require? A fixed rail, a take and a declared input recording are known at build or load. An unforeseen hit exists only from its input tick. A closed form of current inputs can be live and have no memory. A recording can be known in full and require sequential evaluation to bake. Attach availability to graph leaves. For each observed pose root, propagate dependence on time, input stream, previous state and immutable data. A library planner can propose a smaller realization and print its assumptions. A request to bake a dependent live input names the offending leaf and the consumer's alternatives, supplying a recording or executing live. No transformation moves a response before its cause.

One worthwhile specialization follows directly from the pin. For a recorded foot, keep a run of stance as an interval and a support-space point. Store flight endpoints and the author's law where those determine the flight. For arbitrary nonlinear or opaque dynamics keep actual samples or replay the recurrence. A four-second eight-foot take at 120 Hz has 3,848 foot-time samples including the initial pose. Suppose a known take has 23 swings, starts and ends with every foot down, and has no in-stance slip. Then there are 31 stance spans and 23 swing spans across the feet. At 32 bytes per stance and 48 per swing that is 2,096 bytes, plus nine 32-bit offsets for the foot directories, 2,132 bytes total before bundle alignment. The dense foot-only payload is 481 × 384 = 184,704 bytes. This predicts an 86.6-fold reduction for those foot records. Excluded in both are the body path, support transforms, interpolation code and indices outside the specified directory. Each proposed span must reconstruct the same declared trajectory, including retargets in mid-flight. Split at each retarget or preserve dense samples. The real varied scramble's 23 steps alone do not establish that it admits this encoding. [Measured scramble](../../../../_wt/loft-motion/agent_notes/2026-11-19_motion.md)

A compact record can be slower to read than two coalesced dense fetches. Make both lowerings inspectable. For sequential playback a foot can retain its interval cursor. For random access use a bounded search in that foot's ordered interval table. On the illustrative 54-span total, even if all 23 swings concentrate on one foot its list has at most 47 spans, six binary comparisons. Instance copies sharing the same recorded take share its bytes. Shader count follows distinct law bodies and layouts. Table bytes and upload time necessarily grow with unique takes. Compare lowering A and B on the same root, same draw and shuffled times. If the original graph is unavailable for comparison, require the source author to specify the replacement's semantics and record what was measured. Discovering a span pattern in sampled black-box output does not prove it held between samples.

For live work retain the previous causal state and double-buffer it. Inputs take one of two ordinary forms, a sampled value with a declared reconstruction rule or ordered events consumed during the tick window. Drive a tick from its integer index. A host advances four 120 Hz ticks per presentation at 30 Hz and two at 60 Hz. Supply each tick's historical inputs. Under load, a host may carry tick debt or explicitly resynchronize at a supplied checkpoint. It cannot repeat today's impulse over the four missed ticks and call it replay. If a stream lacks history, a backward seek is unavailable before its retained boundary. A checkpoint includes persistent multipliers, controller memory, integrator state, input cursor and stable ordering wherever used. At 448 bytes every 60 ticks the small crab's snapshots occupy 53,760 bytes per minute in steady state, excluding initial snapshot, metadata and input log. A random seek from the nearest earlier checkpoint needs at most 59 steps.

Today's frame identity includes the bundle hash, device, time, size, camera, parameters and current inputs. Histories ending at identical steering values can produce different legs. Extend that same identity for live frames with the initial/checkpoint identity, ordered input-prefix digest through the presented tick, tick and interpolation fraction, and any presentation choice that changes the pose. Immutable logged assets can contribute a content hash. An unrecorded external pose needs identified frame bytes if its picture is to be reproducible. Two distinct earlier hits can lead to identical inputs at display time. Their keys must diverge. On one validated device identical full histories should reproduce frames. Across devices compare image and reported residual tolerances, particularly at branch boundaries where one rounding difference can choose a different support. The author can explicitly quantize a scoring tie if the scene needs cross-device decisions to agree. [Frame identity today](../../../rust/crates/loft-ir/src/identity.rs)

## Performance bets with a price for being wrong

Anchor the estimates to the right observations. On the research branch, the fixed-swing crab baked 480 steps in 3.1 ms GPU, with 309 ms of pipeline setup and about 0.21 MB kept. Its pictured playback at 512² took 0.59 ms at one selected time, best of 60. That timing has neither live steering nor a tail percentile. The Rust base heron solve took 117 ms, the moved deck 221 ms, best of nine under load. They took 309 and 594 steps respectively. ## Inheritance and the competitive claim

Spore supplies the precedent for stating an action in terms of meaningful endpoints on bodies invented later. Spacetime constraints supply directed unknowns and demands over a known future. Position-based methods supply cheap local projections, and XPBD supplies compliance and multiplier updates with an explicit timestep. DeepMimic demonstrates that a tracking policy can recover a performance under disturbance. Use each where the scene benefits. A modeller should be able to identify a semantic grasper, run an imported performance through it, project its contact for one interval and hand its later release to a physical policy, while keeping the rest of the shot hand directed. These techniques and their respective numerical methods are inherited. The work Loft must invent is a compiler and authoring contract that can follow *one directed contact* across these implementations. Use that information to substitute a span or live construction locally, specialize just its reachable graph, and ask the same question of the replacement. Test the idea by making the imported claw bear weight, yield on an impulse, then regrip on a moving handle. The exact hold ends on the tick the consumer chooses. Predict one source law or one data asset per edit, zero compiler changes, useful picture in ten minutes per task on a warm checkout. For capability, score the novel body, interrupted grip and directed glance at game distance, with units and intervals for each claimed hold.
