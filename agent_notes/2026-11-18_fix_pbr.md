# Fix lane: PBR and styles (D9, `--style toon`), 2026-11-18

Brief: `agent_notes/2026-11-18_plan_review/fixes/pbr.md` (D9 of `CONSOLIDATED.md` section 3) and the `--style toon`
bug from `agent_notes/2026-11-18_pbr_style/NOTE.md`.

## What was wrong

- The environment's specular weight was a fixed fit to `ggx` + `smithGgx`, kept silently when either was swapped
  (a lobe of 0 still reflected 0.05 of a uniform sky of 0.5 off a black body; Neumann visibility missed its own
  furnace by 0.076).
- A sky that read `time` or a param was projected at time 0 / its default without a word.
- `pbr({ sky })` evaluated the clear colour on the CPU unconditionally: a GPU-only sky crashed the constructor even
  beside an `environment` that never projects it; the CPU-sky error was a plain `Error` (exit 1).
- Sky scaling by the sun's amount was untested; the furnace test worked around it.
- Styles saw a lamp's arrival only as unshadowed x visibility.
- `--style toon` on a scene whose own toon had another outline (`examples/second/heron.ts`, 0.03) failed:
  `param "toon.outline" declared twice with different defaults (0.03, 0.04)`, exit 1. Cause: params were interned
  process-wide by name, so the replaced style's param, built at import, collided with the new one.

## What changed

- `PbrParts` gains two slots: `lobeAlbedo: (n_v, rough) => { scale, bias }` and `clear: [r, g, b]`. Default
  `lobeAlbedo`: the old fit for the default pair (identity match, so default pictures are byte-identical: 8 views of
  spheres and heron_lamp `--style pbr`, same rgba hashes before/after); for any other pair, the split sum integrated on
  the twin at build (16 x 16 grid, half-vector quadrature stratified by GGX, 32 x 16 points; ~30 ms, cached per pair),
  fitted by a degree 6 x 5 polynomial in sqrt(n.v), rough, checked at grid midpoints and refused over 0.02.
- `PbrError extends SceneError` (exit 4) with variants `varies` (sky/distribution/visibility reading time, a param or an
  input), `gpu-only` (sky/tone/lobe without a CPU twin; the fix names `clear` and `environment`), `unfitted`.
  Decision: refuse, not re-project.
- Convention decided and documented: the sky is radiance per unit of the sun's amount (background, haze, environment
  all scale). Tested at amounts 0, 0.5, 1.
- `src/light.ts`: the fold hands `Arrived` = `Arrival` + `unshadowed` + `visibility`; `amount` stays the product.
- Params interned by (name, default); one scene's two defaults are refused in `src/compile/bind.ts`, as inputs are.
- Docs: `docs/styles.md` (slots, convention, what is built on the CPU, `{ ...pbr(), water, ground }`), `docs/guide.md`
  (water/ground override), `docs/lighting.md` (the fold's new fields), `docs/errors.md` (pbr refusal row),
  `docs/api.md` regenerated.
