Plan: `docs/proposals/2026-11-19_lean_rust_loft.md` §2.2, §2.3, §8.2 lane 0.2. Branch `feat/lr02`.

## What

- `tools/parity/corpus.json`: every `examples/**/*.ts` (125 today; the plan said 124) and the 170 creative seeds
  (crab-actions-v1 103, crab-improve-v1 67, sha256-pinned at creative `b7f79a82`, ported by `tools/migrate.ts`), and
  the §2.2 frame list (3 cameras x {0, 0.5, 1.37, 2.9} s at 512², +6.0 s for `Steps` scenes, one 1280x800 at 1.37 s).
- `tools/parity/run.ts reference`: builds each entry with the real CLI, records the typed refusal (verdict kind, exit,
  site, text) or the whole `Verdict` objects of its checks, and draws frames with `loft-view` (wgpu 30, Metal) as
  lossless PNG. Scenes the build refuses for JS-filled tables (Storm) are drawn on Dawn and marked `dawn` (compared
  under the cross-executor rule). Resumes an interrupted run: R0 is the last commit touching the picture paths, stamped
  in `out/parity/R0/r0`; a mismatch refuses with the fix. Every child has a 300 s deadline (one `loft-view` hung 15 min
  on one frame at load 25; redrawn afterwards byte-identical).
- `tools/parity/run.ts repeat`: draws every reference frame again and measures any difference.
- `tools/parity/diff.rs` (`parity-diff`, standalone crate until lane 3.3 moves it into `loft-draw diff`): §2.2a rules
  1-3 and the §2.3 cross rule, `--envelope N`. Edges come from picture jumps > 32 codes, since `loft-view` writes no
  depth; the report says `"edges":"picture-jump-32"`.

Lines (scc code): 464 of the ~800 ceiling.

## Left

- Integrator: nothing to register (the comparator crate is standalone with its own `[workspace]` and lock, outside
  `rust/`). Lane 3.3 moves `diff.rs` into `loft-draw diff`.
- `serpent_clip` refuses as `internal` on R0: a TS bug, kept as the reference outcome; whoever fixes it records it as a
  deliberate change under §2.2c.
- Edge source is the reference picture until `loft-draw` writes depth.
- Throughput: repeat is serial, about 50 min for 1,479 frames, dominated by one `loft-view` process (device +
  pipelines) per frame. Wider fan-out would not help much on one GPU at load 14; the lever is drawing all of a scene's
  frames in one process (`loft-draw serve`, lane 3.3).
