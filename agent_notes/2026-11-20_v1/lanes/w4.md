## Prediction before build

Encoding a portable hermit currently projects `ModuleBlob` and `TableDecl` into separate manifest schemas, then rebuilds them from those schemas and the bulk sections. Field order and variants for passes, formats and bindings are written once in IR and again in the codec. Put the frozen tags and fields on the IR declarations. Keep the compiler-free codec in Format. A manifest reader will construct modules and tables only when its validated bulk sections supply their bytes. Keep the existing ordered row check and recursive pass limit. Delete `Manifest` and `TableMeta`, and use one array codec across the numeric wire lanes.

Expect approximately 100 fewer physical lines across the owned crates, identical ABI and packed bundle bytes, and the cut's gate scores (Pictures 0/18, Twin 36/36, Truth 34/34, Speed 12/12, Load 31/33). Stable images should have zero changed pixels. OPEN documents nondeterministic depth ties for the three Capsules-20 frames, so compare strict RGBA and identify any differences overlapping the archived witnesses.
