# Phase 2+3 L3 motion standard library

## Prediction before compiling

At 120 Hz, crab input starts at +0.6 m/s and asks for −0.6 m/s on tick 1.
The 6 m/s² cap gives x(n) = 0.005n − n²/4800 and v(n) = 0.6 − 0.05n
until v drops below 0.12 m/s. With h = 0.49 m, the forward capture point
is x + sqrt(h/9.81) v + 0.5(h/9.81)a. Its magnitude crosses the 0.087 m
lift threshold between n = 8 and n = 9. The first quartet should lift on
tick 10 (0.083333 s), and the second by tick 24. The body crosses zero
velocity on tick 13, so the next lift should follow within 11 ticks (0.091667 s).
Each tick uses constant-time strip support and one lift arbitration independent
of the eight rendered legs. Each planted toe is reachable from its socket.

Heron uses 0.77–1.55 s ballistic flight, with exact endpoint positions,
a 0.59 m crouch and a neck with six independently measured links of
1.55/6 m. Boss reaches 0.69 m windup before hit 1, slips under the 420 kg
body's impulse, and takes hit 2 at tick 75 before kinetic friction stops it.
The predicted verdicts are Held at every sample, with the original planted
toe limits of 0.05 mm (crab) and 0.5 mm (heron/boss), 0.2 mm neck error,
and 12° trunk pitch. A 20 mm perturbation of the rendered toe should Miss.

The capture-point estimate, support/CoP clamp, cosine-law IK, XPBD
projection and exact spring are inherited techniques. The four-foot
checkerboard split and one-axis strip are scene-specific reductions.

## Implementation and integration

- `lib/motion.loft` holds the space-aware capture point, quintic, XPBD
  corrections, IK, exact spring and axial squash kernels. The scalar
  quintic returns one axis's position, velocity and acceleration.
- `engines/roam.loft` owns support-limited drive, lift arbitration and toe
  trajectories. `engines/act.loft` owns crouch, closed-form flight and
  Coulomb slip. `lib/{roam,act}.loft` are symlinks for the existing flat
  CLI prelude loader. The engine sources remain independently editable.
- `scenes/{crab,heron,boss}.loft` own the state rows, geometry, beat and law
  schedules, and truth checks. Pin checks call the same IK endpoints as
  the traced pose. The toe-offset mutant changes pose independently of
  the plant anchor.
- `crates/loft-passes/tests/motion_std.rs` builds and validates the scenes
  without a device. The test adapter applies scalar row writes around
  the existing expression twin and evaluates the authored checks with
  the snapshot tag/value encoding. There is no Rust copy of the scene
  controller or verdict condition.

This lane uses the base compiler's executable row-local subset. It leaves
the frozen ABI and goldens untouched. To run the executable scenes through
the final v1 path, the integrator needs BODY/STATE/POSE authoring and reads,
surface pin frames, typed array/record values and the plan table sampler.
That wiring is also needed for the full §7 signatures, arbitrary support
hulls and load shares, FABRIK, joint limits, and the full boss antenna solver.
Heron currently follows an analytic hop score, and boss keeps the planar
impulse and slip in scalar rows. Wire the outbox on the two authored impact
edges once `emit_event` is available. The full GPU acceptance belongs to
the integrator.

`CARGO_BUILD_JOBS=2 tools/dev.sh test loft-passes --test motion_std --no-capture --no-fail-fast`
passed 3/3 after correcting the prelude's FBM argument to a three-component
coordinate and using explicit terminal verdict expressions. The compiler
built three scenes and Naga validated all 16 modules. All eight scheduled
checks held at ticks 0–481 (3,856 verdicts). The test uses each scene's
stage-0 rows and its own scheduled laws. It also checks the displaced toe
at tick zero, before restoring the row for the normal run.
