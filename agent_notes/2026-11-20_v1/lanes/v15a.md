# v15a · World queries in tiled shading

Main `faba061`, legacy `acb7e8b`. Apple M4 Metal, 1280 × 800 at t = 0.
Prediction before profiling: B removes 4,096,000 full-screen primary walks per
frame. At 20 groups, 30,000 estimated edge pixels imply about 120,000 full
walks. Predicted B-20 ≤6 ms and ≥99.9% of RGBA pixels within 2/255 of main.

Inherited Vis64, capsule roots, World BVH, PCG samples and legacy film. Added a
typed shade callback, per-edge four-tap resolve, and center reconstruction of the
stored row. B schedules five GPU passes. Subpixel-only features can escape
the raster-ID halo. R1 hardware traversal needs acceleration-structure
bindings in the bundle and native recorder, outside this lane's files.

Historical p95s (legacy/native, contended) were A-1 10.062/9.573,
A-20 11.000/10.465, B-1 11.202/14.960 and B-20 26.608/58.269 ms.
The two protected preflights measured triangle p95s of 5.621 and 7.051 ms
(limit 0.150), with empty Cargo/rustc checks and 97–100% observed device use.
Both aborted before the paired timings. G1.2 and G1.3 remain open.

A's serialized programs and both RGBA frames matched main exactly.
B's initial and aligned 20-group captures had 2,330 and 6,614 pixels over 2/255.
Reconstructing the stored row in compute reduced B-1/B-20 to 27/483 of
1,024,000 pixels over 2/255, with largest connected clusters of 1/2 pixels.
Legacy-to-native B PSNR was 47.37/37.38 dB (historical 47.26/37.04).
`tools/dev.sh check loft-cli`, lab build, focused callback/bundle test and
`bash -n tools/ab_legacy.sh` passed.
