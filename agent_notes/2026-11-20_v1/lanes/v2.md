## Predictions before building

- The Heron horizon runs once at Stage 0. It prescribes the same 0.59 m
  crouch key at 0.60 s as the old score. With the horizon's 2.4 s duration
  and 96 spans, that time is row 24. Sampling that row directly should
  recover the f32 word for 0.59 m, preserve all 481 displayed pose inputs
  and give zero changed RGBA bytes at the archived 0.5 s frame.
- The motion readback compares every 32-bit word of the packed row, verdict
  and outbox to the Runner's CPU interpreter at ticks 0 through 480.
  Existing GPU tick grouping is expected to remain at 0/481. CPU and GPU
  may differ where Metal contracts the scalar laws' arithmetic or evaluates
  native vector reductions in a different order.
- The Crab dispatch remains one row-local pass. Expect about 0.02 ms p95
  for 1,000 rows, reversal by tick 12, and planted drift below 0.05 mm.

## Verification and blockers

The live Heron integration attempt changed 15 of 1,024,000 archived
pixels. At 0.5 s the hip changed from 0.5992285 to 0.5992284 m.
The source evaluator also panicked on `sample_prior` at `eval.rs:545`,
while the Metal tests passed. The branch retains the bound plan and the
reference playback. All three final 1280×800 RGBA frames at 0.5 s matched
the archives exactly. References were untouched.

The first workspace run was 149/150. The first rerun stopped on the new
fixture's missing Rust import. The second rerun passed all 151 native
tests. No further workspace builds ran.

Applying the independent curve, binding full BODY/POSE, and fixing the
CPU/Metal row and verdict differences remain open. E201 and E308 still
have no emitters or snapshot fixtures. The geometry bound derivation
(`lip.rs`), scan bake (`lib.rs`) and source evaluator (`eval.rs`) are
outside the lane's file ownership.
