# L4 plan

## Prediction before compilation

The scientist's anticipation examples give a 0.589621 m quintic heron crouch and a 0.693209 m quintic boss windup from the endpoint positions, velocities and accelerations. Those quintics are independent references. The solver fits cubic splines, for which squared jerk is constant per span. Giving all 96 uniform midpoints equal weight would count some spans four times and others five. An independent dense equality-constrained calculation gives 0.61232 m and 0.70018 m with those weights. Integrating each constant with its own span length removes that bias. Predict a crouch of about 0.594 m and a windup of 0.69459 m, both within half a centimetre of the published two-decimal targets. The Rust proof will scan the solved curves at 12,001 times and check the actual arrival state, with no key at either extremum.

The flight rows will be affine in takeoff position and velocity and will carry gravity as a known offset. Touchdown position and velocity eliminate the first two following spline controls. Expect C¹ joins, exactly zero flight effort and jerk, and less than 1e-12 m error against a separately evaluated f64 parabola. There are at most 72 scalar unknowns, with a half-bandwidth of at most 17 across the flight substitution, so one band factorization costs fewer than 24,000 multiply-subtracts. Inconsistent holds must return the frozen tag and reason 3 and 5.

The heron used effort weight 1 and jerk weight 0.25. The boss isolated jerk to test the published arrival-only anticipation. Neither fixture supplied a target depth or a windup key. Other tests checked that gravity is the effort minimum, and that an active signed box clearance can move a near-prior target while respecting a position tolerance and the pitch range.

## Files and integration

- `crates/loft-plan/src/lib.rs` is the typed Stage 0 entry point. `spline.rs` assembles cubic derivative rows and substitutes the ballistic segment at C¹ joins. `solve.rs` uses analytical outer-product Hessians, banded Cholesky and a 40-iteration augmented Lagrangian with a line search against the actual signed clearance.
- `crates/loft-plan/tests/plan.rs` contains the two numerical references and solver checks.
- `crates/loft-passes/src/plan.rs` fills the existing lowering stub. `lower` appends a 32-byte header and 97 uniform 48-byte rows to the existing f32 LUT. Offsets are word indices. Integer lanes use the exact u32 bits. Status is in header word 3 and the failure reason remains in the returned `VerdictRecord`. `PRIOR_WGSL` uses the existing LUT binding. The same module supplies the f32 CPU twin and validates the WGSL without a device.

Pass resolved SI objectives and holds to `loft_passes::plan::lower` during Stage 0 and route its verdict into Truth. Surface lowering supplies height and world velocity at the landing time. Clearance receives world-aligned box bounds and is checked at the 96 uniform midpoints. Pitch is the neutral value projected into the intersection of the declared ranges. The lane exposes the typed lowering seam. Connecting surface syntax, scheduling the table consumer and running the authored scenes are integration work. The measured hold residuals apply to the f64 collocation solve. The uniform f32 table is a C¹ playback approximation in cells that cross a phase switch.
