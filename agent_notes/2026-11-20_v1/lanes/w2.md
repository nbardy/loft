## Prediction

At 1280 × 800, t=0, 30 warmups and 100 ring-timed frames, predict a Shrine p95 of 3.95 ms from the archived 4.210 ms. Fresh alpha controls show that removing the lantern or the visible branch draw each saves about 1 ms in median Vis. A separated infinite-cone miss also misses both endpoint balls. The scratch cone guard cut median Vis by 0.128 ms and Shadow by 0.038 ms. Emit the nine PCF taps with the original coordinates and addition order to reduce Shade control flow and expose independent texture requests. Keep the same map and caster world. Compare complete decoded RGBA buffers and retain the pass stamps and Naga counts.

The capsule cull writes survivors at their row indices, with a clipped sentinel for gaps before the last survivor. For equal stored depths, strict Less keeps the first primitive in the ordered instance draw. Predict one hash per time for repeated beauty and ID renders, with changes restricted to the previously observed ties. Multi-draw schedules retain their current cull order.

Apple M4 Metal, close camera at t=0, 1280 × 800. Each run used 30 warmups, 100 ring-timed frames, and the GPU lock. The releases used the same 1024² map resolution, primary and caster worlds, and six passes. The table follows the measured ABBA order.

Disabling the visible branches or lantern in isolated alpha probes reduced median Vis from 2.257 ms to 1.276 or 1.255 ms. CoralBranch had 320 Naga ALU operations, 36 branches and 25 storage loads across entry points. The cone guard skips both cap solves on separated misses. The first patch combined that guard with static PCF taps and scored 4.049 and 4.075 ms p95, with median Shade 0.453 and 0.447 ms. Several late frames incurred an approximately 1 ms spike in Shadow or Classify. The revision split the tetrahedral normal and AO loops for analytic woodblock beauty, keeping the same taps, weights and accumulation order. Shade then reached 0.312 ms p50. Naga's static loop depth fell from two to one, static texture sites rose from two to ten as the nine PCF comparisons became explicit, and CoralBranch's storage loads held at 25. Its ALU/branch counts became 345/35. Static site counts do not measure executed operations through loops or early exits.
