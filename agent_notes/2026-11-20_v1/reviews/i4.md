**FAIL. Pictures rejected all 18 approved frames.** Coral Shrine and the twenty-crab benchmark also missed their speed budgets on a quiet Apple M4. Main stays at `18d3e1adfa0aa8b92754a94e1202c8783bc8bf9a`. L4a, L4b and L4c were combined locally in that order. The candidate and integration repairs are preserved on `loft/i4-scored`. All three lanes are excluded from release on `loft/int4`.

## MUST-FIX

1. **L4b changes the approved appearance.** In the first one-crab frame, 599,245 of 1,024,000 pixels differ by more than 2/255. The allowed count is 1,024. The twenty-crab frame has 511,267 such pixels. A single-frame control using the main version of `lib/bsdf.loft` with the candidate compiler reduced the one-crab count to 299. This isolates the material library as a cause of the rejection. The new GGX compensation passed the white furnace. Both appearance and physical tests are release requirements. All 18 original RGBA hashes matched the approved manifest before and after scoring.
2. **L4a and L4c do not deliver the two-world Shrine.** L4a builds and refits the frozen BVH correctly on the washer and bore fixtures. The submitted L4c shader iterated every instance. The integration now binds the BVH and uses per-kind analytic or marched intersections and any-hit traversal on reference rays. The required `world PrimaryWorld` declaration still exits 1 with E405. The submitted Shrine shades with `sun_sky` and casts branch-only shadows. A solid-lantern snapshot exists on the CPU. The authored preview took 7.713 ms p95 against 4.0 ms. The dual-world woodblock schedule has no valid measurement. See `dual-world.log` and `shrine.json`.
3. **L4b’s authored volume call stops at type-checking.** A valid `inscatter_joint(w,r,1m,24,rng)` gives E405. The GPU marcher agrees with the analytic coincident slabs when supplied their joint coefficients. No authored World sampler supplies those coefficients to a reference path or multiplies its visibility by RGB transmittance. The standard shelf also lacks `sheen` and `transmission`. The SSS lobe uses wrapped diffuse. Hold this lane until the required operations execute in a scene.
4. **L4c reports violations on a valid bound.** At scale two, exactly 12,916 of 12,916 foreground pixels are red and the background has zero red pixels. At scale one the bound is valid, yet 10,216 foreground pixels are red. At scale one-half there are zero. In `emit.rs`, subtracting nearby f32 samples introduces error before `loft_ratio` compares the norm to 1.0. The diagnostic needs a derivative or numerical uncertainty treatment that can distinguish a genuine violation at the specified threshold.
5. **L4c’s native bake does not reach the shared bundle runner.** The submitted code requested 384 invocations after negotiating 256. After fixing that, a separate empty-scene cull bound 16 bytes where its shader requires 32. The integration repairs both validation panics and times the entire load interval. The native fixture now passes, including dirty and clean frames. The frozen `Bundle` has tables and passes but no texture dimensions or bake-clock declarations. The shared runner cannot reconstruct these resources from this bundle. Changing this shared contract needs a serial freeze. The physical Runner count also rises from 1,003 to 1,206 against the 1,000-line cap.
6. **L4c’s transport and post stages are incomplete.** The 1,000-light estimator passes on real device bindings. Its two reservoirs draw proposals at the current hit. Frame history, temporal reprojection and the scheduled bloom mip chain are absent. The reference integrator also treats an unresolved march (−2) as an environment miss. This can introduce light into closed geometry. See `engines/reference.loft` and `crates/loft-passes/src/world_path.wgsl` on the scored branch.
7. **The twenty-crab speed regression is independent of device contention.** The ordinary benchmark measured 5.935 ms p95 against the existing 5.6 ms budget. Its triangle control was 0.076 ms. All seven controls were below the 0.35 ms warning threshold.

## SHOULD-FIX

- `paths.rs` assembles per-kind WGSL by slicing emitted shader text and rewriting identifiers and buffer expressions. `world_path.wgsl` evaluates the field at several independent sites. Lower shared queries at the typed boundary so renamed helpers and alternative schedules retain their meaning.
- `lib.rs` selects two path functions by name and exempts a kernel named `deposit_caustic` from the deferred-lowering guard. Test an independently named user integrator and a kernel with additional deferred operations. Unsupported constructs should retain their own source spans.
- Dirty bakes in the CLI watch scalar words. Track all components of vector parameters such as `sun_dir`. Native resource setup propagates plain strings in several places. Preserve typed capacity, ABI and unsupported-capability errors at the shared backend boundary.
- The spectral study rendered at 128² and four samples. A converged caustic comparison and a material sampling its baked ink texture remain outstanding.
