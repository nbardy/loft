Ordinary arithmetic rounds nearest-even per operation. Explicit FMA rounds once. Lower sin/cos/atan2/acos/exp/log to fixed range reduction and minimax FMA chains. ## D2 · Tier 0 GPU time

**The expensive unit is one opcode dispatch per field sample.** V1’s clipped sphere has ten words, three live vec4s and no loop. A 192-step march plus nine normal/AO taps can decode 2,010 instructions for a single hit. On identical pixels and pipelines the confirmation median premium was 10.000 ms with the full VM, 6.803 ms with four registers, and 0.392 ms with fused operands. I fused the previously missed two-sided clip and snapped its bounds. The same 11 words and pixels went from 2.631 ms upload / +3.273 ms paired p95 to 0.472 / +0.246 and 0.485 / +0.170 ms. A/A was +0.159 ms. [VM](../../../out/meta/d2/slab-vm.log) · [Fused](../../../out/meta/d2/slab-fused.log) · [Repeat](../../../out/meta/d2/slab-fused2.log). [V1 evaluator controls](../../../out/meta/v1/confirm/controls.log)

**Prepare by dependency.** Keep the 56-op wire format, 32 logical vec4 registers, loop targets and reserved IDs. Derive a private execution plan on edit. Fold constants and bounded invariant loops, cache row operands by generation, update frame operands once per frame. Classify the dirty kind into 8×8 tiles. Stage uniform instruction words once per tile in compute, before divergent exits. Visibility fragments use the same predecoded read-only plan. Key row values by row ID even in mixed tiles. Linear-scan live ranges, use named scalar lanes for small blocks (target ≤8 live vec4), and keep the verified 32-register VM for residual graphs. Measure scratch traffic.

Fuse point-dependent straight-line blocks into prewarmed evaluators with parameters in tables. Cover arithmetic, absolute-coordinate clips and nested CSG by structure. Reuse the decode across a pixel’s ray, then evaluate the four normal and five AO taps in two dependent batches. Cache hit results only at the same position, row and generation. Each new march distance still depends on the previous sample. A once-per-pixel *distance* would change the surface. Derive snapped bounds and Lipschitz values compositionally from the fresh compiler’s witnesses. Bound residual cost by covered pixels × visits × unfused ops. Loop trips and sample counts preclude a universal bound for arbitrary tapes. Score the full-screen 63-op control. **Measure whole frames.** Alternating the *same* promoted pipelines at 1280×800 gave +0.086 then +0.980 ms paired frame p95. Present alone gave +0.012 then +0.507 ms. Its archived percentiles cannot isolate clock, queue or predecessor effects. Record raw pass start/end, encode AB/BA pairs into one submission and defer readback. Never subtract pass p95s or discard a slow frame. [First A/A](../../../out/meta/d2/aa.log) · [Repeated A/A](../../../out/meta/d2/aa-repeat.log)

**Prediction and gate.** Budget +0.05 ms staging, +0.19 visibility and +0.06 shade per frame. Predict +0.30 ms paired p95, 0.8 ms source to last upload and zero foreground creates. Require ≤2.0 / +0.5 ms for every edit in three locked runs, fresh-image parity, field error ≤1e−5, ≥99.9% shaded pixels within 1/255, unchanged clean pipelines and generation-safe 400 ms quiet promotion. Predeclare A/A p95 ≤0.10 ms and triangle median ≤0.35 ms. Publish every run. A noisy control leaves the result uncertified.

## D2 · One execution path

**The beauty fault is a missing buffer binding size.** V3 gives every buffer VERTEX usage and binds its entire allocation. Pinned wgpu-core adds a byte for an empty vertex range and aligns the allocation. Metal saw 1,123 f32s in a 1,122-word LUT. GGX locates its energy table at `arrayLength(&luts)−1122`. At capsule pixel (640,400), red HDR went from 0.202026 to 31.484375. Changing only the binding to offset zero and `size=buffer.size()` (4,488 bytes here) restored the HDR, RGBA (150,152,60,255), and every pixel of all 18 frames.
