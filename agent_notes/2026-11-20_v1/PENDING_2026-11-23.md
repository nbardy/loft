## Insights to keep
- **Brief a look as structure plus real reference photos, not as metrics.** "≤2% highlights" plus "albedo variance above X" produced zero highlights and camo noise (div2). Metrics are two-sided guard bands only.
- **The quality came from a lead critique EVERY round against the photo.** Unsupervised rounds drift: Sonnet's otter shape rounds were voted WORSE than an earlier stage (otter2/OWNER_VOTES.md). "A later round is better" is not a safe label.
- **Haiku is good at filling in a proven recipe** (the 16 chitin retextures) **and bad at inventing 3D form** (the otter).
- **Owner votes are noisy on close pairs:** a re-vote of 16 div1/div2 pairs agreed only 9 times. Use repeated votes on a sample, and treat ties and both-bad as labels.
- **Late rounds add little:** blue swimmer rounds 8 and 10 tied the final, and the rust crab's busy round 1 beat its cleaned-up final. Cap rounds at about 6.
- **Same content in a pair:** only the named axis changes. The owner rejected cross-body pairs as texture pairs.
- **Opus subagents were stopped five times by safeguard false positives** (reasoning_extraction), mostly when viewing animal reference photos. Workaround: the builder never opens the photos; the lead compares and critiques.
- **Resource hygiene:** wrap render batches (not agent loops) in `agent-build --gpu`. A render loop in the heavy slot blocked another session for 35 minutes.
