# How much of Loft's TypeScript library can go?

At `a2b227c`, I can account for **7,119 physical lines to replace or delete** in this lane. The proposed replacements have a **2,346-line budget**, for **4,773 net lines saved**. That would take the audited source and six affected test files from **44,038 to 39,265 lines**, a 10.8% reduction. The old Rust loader accounts for 2,632 of the net. Halving this denominator would require another 17,246 net lines. The example scenes alone contain 16,477 lines. A claim to have halved the engine needs an engine denominator, and a claim to have halved this library needs a much larger demonstrated saving.

The best first engine change is to record one *real* scene through the public CLI. The normal build still calls `rec.job(Program.Scene, roots)` at `src/cli/main.ts:238`. Omission of the third argument means empty passes at `src/record/recorder.ts:132`. The recorder already writes draws and passes at `src/record/recorder.ts:323–375`, and the sample supplies them at `src/record/sample.ts:64`. The last recorded corpus result was 232 builds and loads from 295 entries, zero entries with pictures to compare. Deleting legacy files today would therefore remove the only demonstrated path from these scenes to their pictures. See `agent_notes/2026-11-19_lean_rust_draw.md:67–111`.
