# Lean Rust lane 1.3: math (lr13, 19 Nov 2026)

Plan: `docs/proposals/2026-11-19_lean_rust_loft.md` §1.3, §3.4, §8.2 (lane 1.3). Branch `feat/lr13`.

## What

`rust/crates/loft-core/src/{prim,tape,grad,interval}.rs`: 458 scc code lines after review (ceiling 1,700).

- `prim.rs`: `Prim` (53 authored operators, in `src/expr.ts` `Fn` order, which is the recorder's code) with one
  `PRIMS` row each: WGSL spelling, type rule, arity, splat, meaning. A meaning is an `Atom` or an expansion into
  atoms (`mix`, `mod`, `smoothstep`, `clamp`, `step`, `fract`, `saturate`, `inverseSqrt` per component; `dot`,
  `length`, `distance`, `normalize`, `cross` over whole vectors), written as `compileJs` wrote them. `Atom` (40)
  has one `ATOMS` row each: f32 evaluation (`libm`), f32 ulp bound, interval rule, derivative rule. There are no
  optional fields, so an operator without a rule does not compile.
- `tape.rs`: `Ssa` (a straight-line program of `Lit | Leaf | Op(Atom)`), `Ssa::apply` (one operator over component
  lists, with scalars broadcast; this is the entry the decode of lane 1.1/2.x calls), and `run` (f32, lanes
  contiguous). A short leaf vector is a typed `Unbound`.
- `grad.rs`: forward mode. The derivative with respect to one leaf is appended as instructions, so the tape runs it,
  intervals enclose it and `grad` differentiates it again. A structural zero is never written.
- `interval.rs`: f32-padded enclosures (§3.4). The rule gives the exact f64 enclosure, and the fold pads it by the
  atom's ulp bound, 2·ulps·max(|b|·2⁻²³, 2⁻¹⁴⁹). `Unbounded { at, why }` uses the typed `Why` (`DivisorHasZero`,
  `OutsideDomain`, `Pole`, `Overflow`, `Unbound`).

## Pass/fail (`cargo test --release --test it -- --nocapture`, M4, about 3 s)

Fixture: `bun rust/crates/loft-core/tests/it/dump_math.ts > out/lr13/math.json`. It covers 67 cases: 10 cases of
`tests/expr.test.ts`, 4 of `tests/expr.eq.gpu.test.ts`, and one per operator (53), at 64 f32 points each, with
`compileJs` f64 values.

- **(a) PASS.** 61 of 67 cases are within 4 ulps. The six wider cases are listed by name with bounds:
  `op:dot` 152, `op:cross` 47, `op:mod` 8, `expr.test#0` 71, `expr.test#2` 4.5 and `expr.test#3` 202. Every one
  is cancellation: the result is far smaller than its terms. Every case, these included, is within 1e-6 of
  max(1,|want|) (worst measured 7.39e-7, `expr.test#3`; review re-run). Atom ulps against an f64 reference, 200k points each: tanh 2.07, sinh 1.71,
  atan2 1.38, cosh 1.29, and below 1 for the rest. The table's bounds are those plus one (3, and 4 for the
  hyperbolics), and the test holds them.
- **(b) PASS.** 1,000,000 boxes × 16 points: 480k boxes over single atoms and 520k over random 12-operator
  programs, with every register checked. **0 escapes.** 384,625 boxes were `Unbounded` (log, sqrt, a divisor
  through 0, …) and were skipped.
- **(c) PASS.** 26,464 comparisons (2,040 programs, each leaf, 8 points) against f64 central differences, rel 1e-3 of
  max(1,|fd|): 0 failures. 6,496 points were skipped where a kink or jump lay between the samples or the value was
  non-finite.
- **(d) HALF.** Digest `c711752d05cd8265` (FNV-1a over every register of the fixtures and 500 random programs).
  Review (2026-11-18) found the first digest `b3ef5152a651a565` was arm-only: built for x86_64-apple-darwin
  (Rosetta) it printed `fb024a6be2f34ae5`. Two causes, both fixed: (1) Rust `f32::min`/`max` leave the sign of an
  equal zero to the target (aarch64 `fminnm` vs x86 `minss`); `Min`/`Max` now follow JS (-0 < +0). (2) x86 makes a
  fresh NaN negative, aarch64 positive; `run` writes one canonical NaN. The tests' random inputs also used the
  host's `powf`; they now use `libm::pow`. After the fixes x86_64 and aarch64, debug and release, rustc 1.93 and
  1.99 all print `c711752d05cd8265`. **The Linux training host has still not been run.** x86_64 macOS is the
  same codegen and libm path, so it is the likely answer, not the check. Command for the host:
  `bun rust/crates/loft-core/tests/it/dump_math.ts > out/lr13/math.json && cargo test --release --test it d_tape -- --nocapture`.
  It must print `c711752d05cd8265`.

## Not covered (left)

- **LibFn JS twins** (`noise.ts` ×8, `grad.ts` noise gradients ×2, `soft/medium.ts`, `geom/solve.ts` JET): they
  have no Rust slot by design. They become `graphFn` bodies in lane 2.4, and then the tape covers them.
- **`row`, `fetch`, `iterate`** are not tape instructions yet. They need `Fetch { res }` and `Loop` from loft-ir
  (lane 1.1). The `row` case of `expr.test.ts` is excluded for that reason.
- **Affine forms** (`src/interval.ts`'s correlation-keeping half) are not ported. Heron's 52 proved holds (lane 2.3)
  probably need them.
- `pow` with a base interval reaching 0 or below is `OutsideDomain`, and so is an integer exponent.
- `Pow` is C `powf`, not JS `Math.pow`, at the edges: `pow(1, NaN)` and `pow(-1, ±Inf)` are 1 in C and NaN in JS.
  Not in the fixture domains; WGSL leaves `pow` of a negative base undefined anyway.
- (b) skips a box when any register has no finite enclosure (38% of boxes). Atom ulp bounds are measured, not
  proven (CONJECTURE): the interval fold is as sound as those bounds.
- `Ssa` does no interning or folding. Those belong to the arena passes (§1.10).
